Dear Editor in Chief,

Please consider our manuscript, “SparkSense: Condition Monitoring of AC and DC Motors under Operating and Sensor Variability using Motor Profile Guided Edge Anomaly Detection,” for publication in Electrical Engineering.

The manuscript addresses a practical condition monitoring problem in mixed motor fleets: changes caused by motor condition, normal operation, and acquisition disturbance can produce similar signal amplitudes. SparkSense combines AC and DC motor profiles, current and speed context, vibration features, acquisition validity checks, commissioned operating cells, local persistence, and outage tolerant event records. The contribution is a disturbance aware engineering framework that defines when an edge monitor can issue a valid condition alert and when it must report an acquisition problem or abstain.

The study examines three phase induction and series DC motor profiles under matched mechanical and acquisition interventions. It also corroborates the vibration evaluation on independently collected University of Ottawa bearing recordings. The results show why fault sensitivity must be reported together with specificity under gain and noise changes. They lead to a four state operational policy and technical evidence gates for progression from the present engineering prototype to controlled hardware validation and a field pilot.

We believe the manuscript fits the journal’s coverage of electrical machines and drives and its interest in theoretical, computational, and experimental electrical engineering research. The paper is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and has been approved by all authors. The authors declare no competing interests. The data and supplementary research package are identified in the manuscript.

Thank you for considering our work.
