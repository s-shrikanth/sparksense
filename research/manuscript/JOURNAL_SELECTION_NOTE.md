# SparkSense journal route

## Primary recommendation

Springer Electrical Engineering is the strongest current fit. Its scope explicitly includes electrical machines and drives and accepts theoretical, computational, and experimental work. The manuscript is already structured for this route and honestly combines controlled electrical machine studies with independently measured vibration corroboration.

Submission portal: https://submission.springernature.com/new-submission/202/3

## IEEE alternatives

### IEEE Canadian Journal of Electrical and Computer Engineering

This is the most realistic IEEE alternative among the journals reviewed. It covers all areas of electrical and computer engineering, including energy and power systems, control systems, artificial intelligence, and signal processing. Its typical final length of 3 to 10 two column pages is compatible with the current nine page manuscript. Current quartile labels vary by database and year, with recent sources placing it in Q2 or Q3 categories. Confirm the quartile in the database your institution uses immediately before submission.

This route would require restoring IEEE section and reference formatting and checking the current CJECE author template and submission charges before upload.

Journal page: https://ieeecanada.org/publications/

IEEE publication portal: https://publication-recommender.ieee.org/home

### IEEE Access

IEEE Access is a technically suitable broad scope option for an applications oriented engineering study. It uses binary peer review, reports an average acceptance rate near 20 percent, requires its own template, and is fully open access. The 2026 article processing charge is USD 2,160. This option is less financially attractive and does not remove the need for rigorous evidence and restrained claims.

Submission information: https://ieeeaccess.ieee.org/authors/preparing-your-article/

### IEEE Open Journal of the Industrial Electronics Society

The journal covers industrial electronics, instrumentation, controls, and computational intelligence. The topic fits, but the venue is more competitive and fully open access. A stronger hardware validation section would materially improve this route.

Submission portal: https://mc.manuscriptcentral.com/oj-ies

## Decision

Submit to one journal only. The recommended order is Springer Electrical Engineering first, followed by IEEE Canadian Journal of Electrical and Computer Engineering if the Springer submission is declined. IEEE Access is a valid alternative if the article processing charge is acceptable.
